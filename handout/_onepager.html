The four builds

Build 1 · No code · Anyone

The Pocket Expert

A portable AI skill loaded with your standards and policies. Ready for the switch from GPTs and Gems to Plugins and Skills, with a prompt to convert yours.

Saves: answering the same 20 questions
Build 2 · Teachers · SPED

One-Click Parent Updates

Type a quick note next to each student, click one button, and every parent gets a personal email. Contact dates are logged automatically.

Saves: about 2 hours per round
Build 3 · Office · Dept. leads

The Sunday Night Briefing

Every Sunday at 6 PM, your team gets next week's IEPs, 504s, drills, and meetings in one clean email, pulled straight from the shared calendar.

Saves: "Nobody told me."
Build 4 · Admin · Counselors

The Tiered Alert System

Attendance data sends the right email to the right people by tier and logs every contact. Starts in test mode, so families get nothing until you're ready.

Saves: catching kids at 5%, not 30 days

Every build follows one pattern

  • 1Get data
  • 2Loop through it
  • 3Check a condition
  • 4Do something

The only difference between a tool for 30 students and one for 3,000 is more rows.

Before you build

  • Fake data first. Test with your own email.
  • Describe the data. Never paste it into a chat.
  • Always build a fail-safe: "If not found, leave it for me."
  • Check district privacy & FERPA rules first.

Your waste hunt. What three tasks did you repeat by hand last week? Circle the one that fits the pattern. That's your first build.
